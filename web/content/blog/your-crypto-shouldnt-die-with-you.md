## Why This Guide Exists

Chainalysis estimates that roughly 3.7 million BTC — around 20% of the total supply — are effectively lost. Some belong to early adopters who threw away old laptops. But a growing share belongs to people who simply died without a plan.

The crypto industry has spent a decade perfecting ways to keep assets safe from attackers. It has spent almost no time figuring out how to keep assets safe from life itself — from death, illness, accidents, and memory loss.

This guide is for anyone who holds crypto and wants to make sure it doesn't vanish when they do.

## What the Guide Covers

**Recovery playbook** — If you've already lost access to a wallet, the guide walks through practical steps for every scenario: lost passwords with seed phrases intact, locked exchange accounts, and the hardest case — lost seed phrases with no backup.

**The inheritance gap** — Traditional finance has centuries of infrastructure for wealth transfer: wills, trusts, probate courts. Crypto has none of it. The guide explains why self-custody — one of crypto's greatest strengths — becomes a devastating weakness at the moment of death.

**How Miras solves it** — A detailed walkthrough of the 2-of-3 multisig architecture, the claim verification process, the Dead Man's Switch, and the Heir Readiness Kit. Written in plain language, not Solidity.

**Side-by-side comparison** — How Miras stacks up against traditional wills, custodial services, and DIY seed-phrase sharing — across security, trustlessness, cost, and ease of use.

**FAQ** — Answers to the questions we hear most: Can my heir file a false claim? Can the protocol access my funds? What about quantum computing? What if someone already died without a plan?

## Your Next Steps

If you still have access to your wallets, the guide recommends five concrete actions:

1. Set up a Miras inheritance plan at [miras.global](https://miras.global) so your assets have a trustless path to your chosen heirs
2. Create durable physical backups of your seed phrase and store copies in at least three separate locations
3. Move significant holdings to a hardware wallet purchased directly from the manufacturer
4. Enable hardware-key 2FA and use a password manager for every crypto-related account
5. Share the Miras Heir Readiness Kit with your designated heir so they know exactly what to do

The goal isn't perfect security — it's layered protection that makes loss nearly impossible and inheritance automatic.
